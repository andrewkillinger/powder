FractalUpkeep

A meditative art game of creation and entropy

Unity 2D • iOS

Paint beautiful fractals on a living canvas while battling the relentless fade of decay. Deploy automation units, unlock new tools, and master the balance between creation and entropy.

A unique blend of idle mechanics and artistic expression, FractalUpkeep challenges you to maintain beauty in a world that constantly fades away.

Unity 2022 LTS RenderTexture Canvas Particle System ScriptableObjects iOS Native
🎨

Particle-Ink Brushing

Touch to paint with particles that deposit pigment onto the canvas. Adjust brush size, emission rate, and colors.

⏳

Fade Decay

The canvas constantly fades over time. Counter entropy through painting and strategic automation.

🌱

Automation Units

Deploy Seeds (stationary emitters) and Moths (repair units) to help maintain your art.

💎

Dual Currency

Earn Pigment from beauty and Spores from level completion. Upgrade and unlock new content.

🔒

Seal Mechanic

Temporarily protect areas from decay with strategic seal placement.

📸

Gallery System

Capture snapshots of your completed artwork. Build a collection of your creations.
